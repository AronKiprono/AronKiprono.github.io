Engineering journey

From practical software problems to secure systems.

Computer Science was not my original plan, but it became a field I genuinely loved. What started as an alternative path grew into a practical way to understand systems, solve problems, and build tools that people could rely on. One of the early turning points in my journey was working with friends on a coffee cooperative management system that helped local cooperative societies manage farmer deliveries, loans, processing records, and payroll. That experience showed me that software could go beyond automation and solve real operational problems for people and institutions.

Since then, I have grown through software development, senior engineering, and technical leadership responsibilities. My work with backend systems, Linux infrastructure, deployments, monitoring, and production support has naturally drawn me toward cybersecurity, especially secure systems, authentication, access control, incident response, and application security.
